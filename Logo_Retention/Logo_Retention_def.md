# Logo Retention

*Alternative Name: Customer Logo Retention*

## Definition

Logo Retention, or Customer Logo Retention, measures the percentage of customers that are retained as customers over a specific period of time.

## Business Value and Insights

Logo Retention functions as a foundational retention indicator that excludes revenue considerations. This approach offers value by revealing customer base stability without interference from high-value accounts that might otherwise obscure underlying churn patterns.

## Calculation Formula

$$\frac{\text{Number of customers at the end of the period who were customers at the beginning of the period}}{\text{Number of active customers at the beginning of the measurement period}}$$

### Data Inputs Required

Data Input #1: Number of customers at the beginning of the period. The number of customers should be limited to include those customers that are currently on contract and generating revenue. Freemium, Free Trial and Trial customers are excluded.

Data Input #2: Number of customers at the end of the period. The number of customers should be limited to include those customers that are currently on contract and generating revenue. Freemium, Free Trial and Trial customers should not be included.

Data sources typically include Customer Relationship Management systems, subscription billing systems, and contract management systems.

### Calculation Timing

The metric is typically calculated every month for the prior 12 month period.

## Nuances to Consider

### Nuance #1

Judgment calls may arise in determining churn criteria. For instance, customers current on invoices with a verbal renewal commitment may lack signed renewal contracts by the deadline.

### Nuance #2

Usage-based companies encounter variable customer usage patterns, including zero-usage months. Standard organizational policies should clarify churn classification criteria.

### Nuance #3

Organizations must establish standardized edge-case rules and consistently apply them with proper disclosure.

## Sample Calculations

### Sample Calculation #1

#### List of Input Values

Number of Customers on January 1, 2022 = 350
Number of Customers from Jan 1, 2022 still active on January 1, 2023 = 320

#### Calculation Formula

$$\frac{320 \text{ Active Customers from 1/1/22}}{350 \text{ Active Customers on 1/1/22}} = 91.4\% \text{ Logo Retention Rate}$$
