# Expansion CAC Ratio

## Definition

Expansion CAC Ratio measures the amount of fully burdened Sales and Marketing expenses, including Customer Success expenses, that is required to generate one dollar ($1) of Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue (CARR) from existing customers via Up-Sells, Cross-Sells or Expansion.

The metric includes expansion CARR from upsells and cross-sells but excludes the impact of churn on CARR. Expansion CARR differs from “Net New ARR.”

## Business Value and Insights

This ratio provides visibility into how efficiently CARR grows from existing customer accounts. It supports better resource allocation decisions when considering investment increases for expansion CARR growth.

Expansion ARR encompasses new CARR from upsells, cross-sells, and contractual expansion of billable units within subscription agreements.

Measuring existing customer expansion efficiency becomes increasingly important as SaaS companies surpass $5M ARR and/or develop upsell or cross-sell capabilities. While the calculation formula matters, consistent measurement of expansion efficiency proves equally vital.

## Calculation Formula

$$\frac{\text{Fully Loaded Sales and Marketing Expenses allocated to Expansion}}{\text{Expansion CARR}}^1$$

<sup>1</sup> Sales and Marketing expenses should align with the sales cycle length preceding new ARR generation. For a ~90-day sales cycle, the Q2 Expansion CAC Ratio uses Q1 Sales & Marketing divided by Q2 Expansion CARR. For a ~30-day cycle, use the prior month's Sales & Marketing divided by the current month's Expansion ARR.

### Data Inputs Required

Data Input #1: Fully Loaded Sales and Marketing Expenses (Allocated Customer Success is applicable). This information typically appears on the Income Statement.

Data Input #2: Percentage of Sales, Marketing and Customer Success expenses allocated to existing customer expansion.

Data Input #3: Existing Customer Expansion ARR. This data typically comes from Customer Relationship Management Software, Contract Management Platform, or Financial Management Platform.

### Calculation Timing

This metric should be calculated monthly using rolling 1/3/6/12 month periods.

## Nuances to Consider

### Nuance #1: Fully loaded Sales and Marketing Expenses

Expenses should encompass variable compensation, bonuses, benefits, and allocated shared expenses. Sales commissions require full upfront burden inclusion.

### Nuance #2: Timing of Sales Commissions

If sales commissions represent a meaningful percentage of CAC, a more accurate calculation can be achieved by NOT lagging commission expenses and including them in the same period the new CARR is booked. This approach differs from current ASC 606 Income Statement guidance.

### Nuance #3: Customer Success Expense

Customer Success costs should be incorporated when CS time supports upsells and cross-sells. The allocation should reflect time spent expanding CARR with existing customers. Refer to your company's allocation methodology for Customer Success operating expenses versus Cost of Goods Sold.

### Nuance #4: Time period of Expenses versus CARR

The Marketing and Sales Expenses should be measured for the time period preceding the expansion ARR by the length of the sales cycle.

Example #1: For a ~90-day sales cycle, the Q2 Expansion CAC Ratio equals Q1 Sales & Marketing divided by Q2 Expansion CARR.

Example #2: For a ~30-day sales cycle, use the prior month's Sales & Marketing divided by the current month's Expansion CARR.

## Sample Calculations

### Sample Calculation #1: Expansion CAC Ratio - 90 Day Sales Cycle

#### List of Input Values

Sales and Marketing Expenses allocated to Expansion CARR (91-180 Days ago): $200,000
Customer Success Expenses allocated to Expansion CARR (91-180 days ago): $100,000
Expansion CARR - Last 90 Days: $500,000

#### Calculation Formula

$$\frac{\$200{,}000 + \$100{,}000}{\$500{,}000} = \$0.60 \text{ Expansion CAC Ratio}$$

### Sample Calculation #2: Expansion CAC Ratio - 30 Day Sales Cycle

#### List of Input Values

Sales and Marketing Expenses allocated to Expansion CARR (Last 30 Days): $100,000
Customer Success Expenses allocated to Expansion CARR (Last 30 Days): $50,000
Expansion CARR - Last 30 Days: $300,000

#### Calculation Formula

$$\frac{\$100{,}000 + \$50{,}000}{\$300{,}000} = \$0.50 \text{ Expansion CAC Ratio}$$
